📋 How to Play

  • Mouse: Move your mouse up/down to control the left paddle
  • Arrow Keys: Press ⬆️ ⬇️ to move the paddle
  • Touch: Drag your finger on mobile devices
  • Space Bar / Click: Start/Pause the game
  • Reset Button: Reset scores and start fresh
  • Objective: Keep the ball in play and bounce it past the computer!
  • Ball Speed: The ball accelerates slightly with each paddle hit
